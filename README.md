## Government of Canada’s Call for Information on Advancing AI Transparency
Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada has opened a public consultation on AI transparency. 

https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/have-your-say-advancing-ai-transparency-canada

Consultation period: 23 July – 23 September 2026

The federal consultation asks where transparency matters across five areas:

* AI-generated content
* knowing when you're interacting with AI
* information about AI systems
* AI incidents
* AI agents that can take action on your behalf

In this Lab we're putting the actual questions on the table.
Not a panel. Not another vague conversation about “trust.” A working civic policy lab.

​What we'll do:

​1. Break down, scrutinize, debate, and form opinions on the five federal questions

​2. Decide what BC should tell Ottawa

---
Response to this call can be via email or by doing an online survey. BC + AI will put together a document for email response.
